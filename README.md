# Analyzing AI Jobs Impact (2010–2025)

📊 **Python + Power BI Project**  
Analyzing how Artificial Intelligence adoption impacts jobs, salaries, skills, and reskilling needs across industries and regions.

## business problem 
•  Between 2010 and 2025, Artificial Intelligence (AI) has changed the way people work. Some jobs have disappeared because tasks were automated, while new jobs have been created that need different skills. 
The challenge is to understand which industries gained jobs, which lost jobs, and how skill requirements have shifted. By studying this, we can see the overall impact of AI on employment and help workers, companies, and 
governments prepare for the future.

## 🎯 Objectives

•Track job demand trends (2010–2025)  
• Identify industries leading AI adoption  
• Measure salary growth and automation risk  
• Highlight reskilling needs across countries  
• Provide recruiter‑friendly insights .

📊 Performance Insights:

Job Demand  
• Steady growth in postings from 2010 → 2025, with Tech & Finance leading adoption.
• Top roles: ML Engineer, AI Researcher, Data Scientist — consistently high demand.

Reskilling Needs  
• ~32% of jobs require reskilling → strong signal for workforce adaptation.
• Countries like Netherlands, Chile, Ghana show the highest reskilling percentages.

Salary Trends  
• Average salary doubled from ~$40K (2010) → ~$80K (2025).
• Finance and Tech industries show the strongest salary growth.
• Senior roles capture the highest AI intensity, linking adoption to pay.

Risk & Adoption 
• AI Risk Index ~1.98 → moderate displacement risk across industries.
• Mature adoption is still low (~6%), meaning most industries are in Emerging/Growing stages.
• Global adoption uneven: North America & Europe more mature, Africa & Asia still growing.

---

📌 Conclusion: AI Jobs Impact (2010–2025):

• Job Demand Growth → AI‑related roles (ML Engineer, AI Researcher, Data Scientist) show steady demand increase from 2010 to 2025.

• Reskilling Needs → 32% of jobs require reskilling, with countries like Netherlands, Chile, and Ghana showing the highest adaptation pressure.

• Salary Trends → Average salaries doubled from ~$40K (2010) to ~$80K (2025), with Finance and Tech industries leading growth.

• Industry Adoption → Only ~6% of industries are in the Mature stage, meaning most are still Emerging or Growing.

• Risk Insights → AI Risk Index of 1.98 indicates moderate displacement risk, highlighting the need for proactive workforce planning.

• Company Impact → Firms like Future Analytics and Future Technologies dominate salary spend, reflecting concentrated investment in AI talent.
